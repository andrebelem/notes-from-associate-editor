# Prompts for Figure Generation (Gemini)

This document contains the prompts used to generate the figures in this repository, based on conceptual designs derived from the author's experience as an Associate Editor.

---

## Figure 1 — Editorial Workflow

**File:** figures/workflow_editorial.png

**Prompt version:** v1.0

Hand-drawn notebook style diagram showing the daily editorial workflow of a scientific journal Associate Editor.

The image looks like a page from a scientist's notebook with black ink pen sketches, arrows, boxes, and handwritten notes.

Title at the top:
"Associate Editor – Daily Editorial Workflow"

Main flowchart starting with:
"Manuscript Submission"

Arrows leading through steps such as:

"Scope Check (journal fit)"
"Initial Read"
"Background Check on Authors"
"Conflict of Interest Screening"
"Search Academic Profiles (Google Scholar / ORCID)"
"Check Collaboration Networks"
"Identify Potential Reviewers"
"Invite Reviewers"
"Reviews Arrive"
"Conflicting Reviews?"
"Editorial Evaluation"
"Decision Recommendation"

Next to the "Background Check" step include a small checklist with handwritten items:
- publication history
- co-authorship network
- institutional links
- possible conflicts of interest

Add small visual doodles indicating routine work:
coffee cup, laptop, calendar icon, and a small note saying:
"Daily editorial work"

The drawing should look like realistic hand-drawn scientific notes, slightly imperfect lines, arrows and circles, minimalist academic sketch style.

Notebook paper texture, black ink pen drawing, clean but informal, like the working notes of an Associate Editor reviewing manuscripts.

No people in the image.

## Figure 2 — Editorial Workflow with AI Support

**File:** figures/workflow_ai_support.png

**Prompt version:** v1.0

Hand-drawn scientific workflow sketch on a notebook page, similar to a scientist’s notes, showing the editorial process of an Associate Editor with AI support layers.

Title at the top:
"Associate Editor – Editorial Workflow with AI Support"

Main flowchart (same structure as a traditional editorial process):

"Manuscript Submission" →
"Scope Check (journal fit)" →
"Initial Read" →
"Background Check on Authors" →
"Conflict of Interest Screening" →
"Search Academic Profiles (Google Scholar / ORCID)" →
"Check Collaboration Networks" →
"Identify Potential Reviewers" →
"Invite Reviewers" →
"Reviews Arrive" →
"Conflicting Reviews?" →
"Editorial Evaluation" →
"Decision Recommendation"

Now add AI assistance annotations in a subtle way:

Next to some boxes, include small handwritten notes like:

Near "Scope Check":
"AI: scope suggestion / mismatch alert"

Near "Background Check":
"AI: publication patterns / network signals"

Near "Identify Potential Reviewers":
"AI: reviewer suggestions"

Near "Reviews Arrive":
"AI: highlight inconsistencies"

Near "Editorial Evaluation":
"AI: flag attention points"

Add small icons (like ⚡ or "AI") next to these notes.

Style:

- black ink pen drawing
- notebook paper texture
- slightly imperfect hand-drawn lines
- arrows, boxes, handwritten annotations
- minimalist academic sketch style
- no people
- include small doodles like a coffee cup and laptop
- include a small note: "daily editorial work"

Very important:
The AI elements should look like annotations or assistance, not replacing the workflow.
